Privacy
notice.

The boring page. It's short because there isn't much to say: no cookies, no tracking, no newsletter you never asked for.

Who is responsible

Diego Pignattini, freelance web developer, Rome, Italy. VAT IT17017061007. I'm the data controller, which is the legal way of saying: if something happens to your data here, it's on me.

Anything about your data: privacy@donotpissmeoff.com.

What I collect

When you send the contact form

Your name, your email address, the type of project you picked and whatever you write in the brief. Nothing is sent until you press the button. You don't have to give me any of it, but without a name and an email I can't reply.

When you load a page

The web server keeps a technical log of each request: date and time, the page, your browser type, and your IP address with the last part cut off, so it points to a network and not to you.

If you email an address at this domain, the mail server logs the sender, the recipient and the time of delivery. Not the content.

The contact form also remembers the full IP address of whoever submits it for up to 24 hours, in memory only, to stop one sender from flooding it. It is never written to disk.

What I don't collect

  • No cookies. That's why there is no cookie banner.
  • No analytics, no ad pixels, no fingerprinting.
  • No fonts, scripts or images loaded from other companies: everything on this site comes from this server.

Why, and on what legal basis

  • Contact form: to read your request, reply and, if it makes sense, send you a quote. You asked for it, so the basis is taking steps at your request before a contract (art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Server and mail logs, and the anti-flood limit: to keep the site running and keep abuse out. The basis is my legitimate interest in a site that works (art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

No marketing, no profiling, no automated decisions about you. I don't sell or rent data to anyone.

Where it goes

  • The server. The site runs on a server I rent from OVH, located in Canada. The European Commission recognises Canada as a country with adequate data protection for commercial organisations.
  • My assistant. A message sent through the form is handed to an automated assistant that runs on that same server. It notifies me and files the message in my private notes. That's the only thing it does with it.
  • Email. Mail sent to an address at this domain arrives on that same server, which forwards it to my personal mailbox at Apple iCloud and keeps no copy. If I reply, the conversation continues from there.

Nobody else gets it, unless a law or a court requires it.

How long I keep it

  • Messages that don't become a project: deleted within 6 months of our last exchange.
  • Messages that do: kept for as long as we work together, and afterwards for as long as Italian tax and civil law requires me to keep contracts and invoices (10 years).
  • Server logs: the web log files are capped in size and the mail log is rotated weekly; the oldest entries are deleted automatically as new ones come in.

Your rights

You can ask me what I hold about you, get a copy, have it corrected or deleted, ask me to restrict how I use it, or object to it. Write to the address above and I'll answer within a month. Usually much faster.

If you think I'm handling your data badly, you can complain to the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, or to the authority of the EU country you live in. I'd appreciate hearing it first, but that's your call.

Changes

If the way this site handles data changes, this page changes with it, and so does the date below.
