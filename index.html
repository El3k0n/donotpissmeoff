    You write down what you need. I ask the questions you didn't think of. We agree on price and date.

  2. Step 2

Before
you ask.

Answers to the questions that would otherwise become a call.

How fast is fast?

Depends on the project. You get a delivery date before we start, and I hit it. If I can't, you hear it from me first, not from the calendar.

How much does it cost?

Fixed price, agreed up front. No hourly meter running in the background, no surprise invoice at the end. Tell me your budget and I'll tell you what fits in it.

Do you do logos or branding?

No. I'm a developer. Bring your brand and I'll make it work on a screen. If you don't have one yet, sort that out first and come back.

Can we jump on a call?

One. Short. With an agenda. Everything else goes in writing, so nobody has to remember who said what.

How many revisions do I get?

Two rounds, included in the price. Send everything in one list. After that, Mo starts counting and so does the invoice.

Do you work outside Italy?

Yes. I'm in Rome, the internet is everywhere. I reply in English and Italian. Mo only coos.
